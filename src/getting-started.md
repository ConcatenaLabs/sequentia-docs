# Getting started

Ten minutes from nothing to your first transaction.

## 1. Get a wallet

Pick one:

- **The web wallet**, at [sequentiatestnet.com/wallet](https://sequentiatestnet.com/wallet/).
  Nothing to install. Choose **Create new wallet**, write down the twelve words it shows, and
  confirm them. The words are your **recovery phrase**: anyone who has them can spend from
  the wallet, and with them you can restore it anywhere. See
  [The web wallet](web-wallet.md).
- **Sequentia Core**, the full node with its desktop wallet, from
  [sequentiatestnet.com/download/core](https://sequentiatestnet.com/download/core/), for
  Linux or Windows. It checks every block itself. On first start, press **Create a new
  wallet** and save a copy with **File → Backup Wallet**. See
  [Sequentia Core](sequentia-core.md).

Keep your recovery phrase or wallet backup private. Never type it into a website, a form
or a message, including a problem report.

## 2. Get testnet coins

In the web wallet, the **Receive** tab asks the faucet for you. Otherwise copy an address
(in Sequentia Core, **Receive → Create new receiving address**), open the
[faucet](https://sequentiatestnet.com/faucet), paste it, choose an asset and send. The
coins arrive within a minute. See [The faucet](faucet.md).

## 3. Look it up

Paste your address into the [explorer](https://sequentiatestnet.com/explorer/) to see the
payment that funded it: the amount, the asset and the block it went into.

## 4. Use it

- **Send** an asset to a friend's address or receive code. The fee can be paid in the
  asset you send.
- **Trade** one asset for another in the web wallet's **Swap** tab.
- **Issue** an asset of your own.
- **Stake** or **delegate** tSEQ: see [Staking pools](staking-pools.md).
- **Bring in** an asset from Ethereum or Solana with [Compages](compages.md).

## 5. Get rewarded for testing

[Emissio](emissio.md) pays Sequence tokens at mainnet launch for completing testnet tasks
and for problems you find. Create an account there first, so you can paste your account
code into the reports you send.
