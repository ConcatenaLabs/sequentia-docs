# Emissio

[Emissio](https://sequentiatestnet.com/emissio/) is the Sequentia community rewards
programme. You earn Sequence tokens (SEQ) for work on the testnet: completing tasks,
winning competitions, and reporting problems and vulnerabilities. Your balance is paid on
the Sequentia mainnet at launch, to a mainnet address you give.

## Join

1. **Register** with an email address and a password, and confirm the email.
2. On **Account**, note your **account code** and your **proof decimals**. Paste the
   account code into a problem report you send from the web wallet, so it is credited to
   you.
3. Verify a social account. You can earn without one, but the launch payout needs it.
4. Add your **mainnet address** (it starts `bc1`) before launch. The **Address guide**
   explains how to make one safely with any well-audited Bitcoin wallet; use a fresh
   recovery phrase for it.

## Earn

- **Tasks** cover every part of the testnet: the web wallet, the faucet, Sequentia Core,
  the explorer, Compages, the staking pools and Emissio itself. Each task says what to do and what
  evidence to submit.
- **Proof that a transaction is yours.** Your account has **proof decimals**: eight
  digits shown on the Account page. A transaction you submit as evidence must have a
  transparent output whose amount ends in exactly those digits, for example
  `1.<your proof decimals>` tSEQ.
- **Bug reports** are rewarded by severity once a reviewer accepts them. Report from
  Emissio's **Bugs** page, or from the web wallet with **Report a problem**.
- **Security reports** go through the **Security** page, privately. Do not disclose a
  vulnerability publicly before it is fixed.
- **Competitions** have their own prizes and deadlines.

Every reward is reviewed before it is credited. One account per person; the **Program
rules** page lists the rest.
